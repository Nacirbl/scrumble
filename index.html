How to Play:

Objective: Be the player with the highest Builder score after two phases.

Phase 1: You are the Builder, your opponent is the Saboteur.
Phase 2: Roles switch! You become the Saboteur, opponent is the Builder.

Gameplay: Players take turns placing one of their 10 letter tiles onto an empty square on the 5x5 board. Try to form 5-letter words (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) using adjacent tiles. Words must be in the game's dictionary.

Scoring (for the current Builder):

  • +1 point for each of the Builder's own tiles in a valid word.
  • +2 points for each of the Saboteur's tiles used in that Builder's word.
The Saboteur aims to block the Builder or trick them into using Saboteur tiles (which give the Builder more points, but might be a trap!).

Game End & Winning: Each phase ends when all 20 tiles for that phase have been placed. The player with the higher total Builder score from their turn as Builder wins. If scores are tied, the player who formed their first word using fewer of their own tiles (in their Builder phase) wins.
