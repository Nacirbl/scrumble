How to Play

Objective: Have the higher Builder score after two phases. Each player builds once and sabotages once.

Phases: In phase 1 you are the Builder and your opponent is the Saboteur. In phase 2 the roles swap.

Gameplay: On your turn you place one of your 10 letter tiles on an empty square of the 5x5 board. Each phase ends when all 20 tiles are placed.

Words: A word forms when a full row, column, or main diagonal spells a dictionary word, read in either direction. The word scores as soon as its fifth tile lands, no matter which letter of it you placed last.

Scoring (for the current Builder):

  • +1 point for each of the Builder's own tiles in a valid word.
  • +2 points for each of the Saboteur's tiles used in that Builder's word.
  • Each distinct word scores once per phase, but one placement can complete several different words at once and they all count. The Found Words panel lists each word and its points; the checkbox shows the path on the board.

The Saboteur: earns +1 "Blocked" point for each of their placed tiles that ends the phase inside no word. Their best moves jam the board without handing the Builder a completion.

Game end & winning: The player with the higher Builder score across their two phases wins. If the scores are tied, the player whose first word contained fewer of their own tiles wins.

How to play Saboteur

  1. Pick a tile from your rack, then click an empty square. You have 10 tiles per phase, and there are two phases.
  2. A full row, column, or main diagonal that spells a dictionary word scores for the Builder: +1 per own tile, +2 per opponent tile. Words count read from either end.
  3. You build in phase 1 and sabotage in phase 2, so a tile that helps you now can hurt you later.
  4. The Saboteur earns +1 for every placed tile that stays out of all words. The Found Words panel lists each word; its checkbox draws the path on the board.
